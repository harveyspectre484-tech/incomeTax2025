[x=  27.62] [y=  65.20] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] Definition of "tax year".
[x=  27.62] [y=  80.59] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] 3. (1) For the purposes of this Act, "tax year" means the twelve months period of the financial year commencing on the 1st April.
[x=  27.62] [y=  96.50] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (2) In the case of a business or profession newly set up, or a source of income newly coming into existence in any financial year, the tax year shall be the
[x=  27.62] [y= 109.23] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] period beginning with—
[x=  40.80] [y= 123.55] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (a) the date of setting up of such business or profession; or
[x=  40.80] [y= 134.16] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (b) the date on which such source of income newly comes into existence,
[x=  27.62] [y= 145.83] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] and ending with the said financial year.
