[x=  27.62] [y= 111.88] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] 4. (1) Where any Central Act enacts that income-tax shall be charged for any tax year at any rate or rates, income-tax for such tax year shall be charged at
[x=  27.62] [y= 124.61] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] that rate or those rates in accordance with and subject to the provisions of this Act.
[x=  27.62] [y= 139.99] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (2) The charge of income-tax under sub-section (1) shall be on the total income of the tax year of every person as per the provisions of this Act.
[x=  27.62] [y= 155.91] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (3) Income-tax shall also include any additional income-tax, by whatever name called, levied under this Act.
[x=  27.62] [y= 171.29] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (4) If this Act provides that income-tax is to be charged in respect of income of a period other than the tax year, it shall be charged accordingly.
[x=  27.62] [y= 186.67] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (5) For the income chargeable under this section, income-tax shall be deducted or collected at source or paid in advance as provided under this Act.
