# Income Tax Department
Ministry of Finance, Government of India

# CHAPTER XXII

# OFFENCES AND PROSECUTION
18[Contravention of order made during search action.]
473. Whoever contravenes any order referred to in section 247(4) shall be punishable with 19[simple imprisonment up to two years and with fine].
18. Sub. for "Contravention of order made under section 247." by Act No. 4 of 2026, w.e.f. 1-4-2026.
19. Sub. for "rigorous imprisonment which may extend to two years and shall also be liable to fine" by Act No. 4 of 2026, w.e.f. 1-4-2026.
