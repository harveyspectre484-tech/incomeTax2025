# Income Tax Department
Ministry of Finance, Government of India
Penalty for failure to furnish report under section 172.
447. 11[***]
11. Omtt. by Act No. 4 of 2026, w.e.f. 1-4-2026. Prior to its omission, section 447 read as under :
"447. Penalty for failure to furnish report under section 172.—If any person fails to furnish a report from an accountant as required by section 172, the Assessing Officer may impose a penalty of Rs. 100000 on such person."
