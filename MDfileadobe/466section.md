# Income Tax Department

# Ministry of Finance, Government of India

# Penalty for failure to comply with the provisions of section 254.
466. If a person fails to comply with the provisions of section 254, the Joint Commissioner, Deputy Director or Assistant Director or the Assessing Officer, may impose a penalty which may extend up to Rs. 13[25000] on him.
13. Sub. for "1000" by Act No. 4 of 2026, w.e.f. 1-4-2026.
