[x=  27.62] [y=  80.59] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] 234. (1) Subject to the provisions of this Part, the tonnage tax scheme shall not apply where a tonnage tax company is a party to any transaction or
[x=  27.62] [y=  93.32] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] arrangement which amounts to an abuse of the tonnage tax scheme.
[x=  27.62] [y= 109.23] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (2) For the purposes of sub-section (1), a transaction or arrangement shall be considered an abuse, if the entering into or the application of such transaction
[x=  27.62] [y= 121.96] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] or arrangement results, or would but for this section have resulted, in a tax advantage being obtained for—
[x=  40.80] [y= 136.28] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (a) a person other than a tonnage tax company; or
[x=  40.80] [y= 146.89] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (b) a tonnage tax company in respect of its non-tonnage tax activities.
[x=  27.62] [y= 158.56] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (3) For the purposes of this section, "tax advantage" includes—
[x=  40.80] [y= 172.88] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (a) the determination of—
[x=  71.75] [y= 183.49] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (i) the allowance for any expense or interest; or

[x=  27.62] [y= 246.08] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (4) Where a tonnage tax company is a party to any transaction or arrangement referred to in sub-section (1), the Assessing Officer shall, by an order in
