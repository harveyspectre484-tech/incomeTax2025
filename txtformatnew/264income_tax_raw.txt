[x=  27.62] [y=  65.20] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] Scheme for submission of returns through tax return preparers.
[x=  27.62] [y=  80.59] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] 264. (1) The Board may make a Scheme for furnishing returns of income through a tax return preparer and such Scheme shall be notified, which—
[x=  40.80] [y=  95.44] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (a) may enable any specified class or classes of persons in preparing and furnishing returns of income through a tax return preparer authorised to act as
[x=  55.20] [y= 104.99] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] such under the Scheme;
[x=  40.80] [y= 115.60] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (b) may be made irrespective of provisions of section 263.
[x=  27.62] [y= 127.26] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (2) For the purpose of this section,—
[x=  40.80] [y= 141.59] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (a) "tax return preparer" means any individual, not being a person referred to in section 515(3)(a)(ii) or an employee of the "specified class or classes of
[x=  55.20] [y= 151.13] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] persons", who has been authorised to act as a tax return preparer under the Scheme made under this section;
[x=  40.80] [y= 161.74] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (b) "specified class or classes of persons" means any person, other than a company or a person, whose accounts are required to be audited under section
[x=  55.20] [y= 171.29] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] 63 or under any other law, who is required to furnish a return of income under this Act.
[x=  27.62] [y= 182.96] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (3) Every notification for the Scheme referred to in sub-section (1) shall be issued as per section 534 of this Act.
