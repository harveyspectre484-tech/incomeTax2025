[x=  27.62] [y=  65.20] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] Special provision for computation of capital gains in case of slump sale.
[x=  27.62] [y=  80.59] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] 77. (1) Any profits or gains arising from the slump sale effected in the tax year shall be chargeable to income-tax as long-term capital gains and shall be
[x=  27.62] [y=  93.32] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] deemed to be the income of the tax year in which the transfer took place, subject to the provisions of sub-section (2).
[x=  27.62] [y= 109.23] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (2) The profits and gains arising from a slump sale involving the transfer of a capital asset, being one or more undertakings or divisions owned and held by an
[x=  27.62] [y= 121.96] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] assessee for thirty-six months or less, immediately before the date of its transfer, shall be treated as short-term capital gains.
[x=  27.62] [y= 137.34] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (3) In relation to capital assets, being an undertaking or division transferred by way of slump sale,—
[x=  40.97] [y= 151.66] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (a) the "net worth" of the undertaking or division shall be deemed to be the cost of acquisition and the cost of improvement for sections 72 and 73; and
[x=  40.87] [y= 164.39] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (b) the fair market value of the capital assets on the date of transfer, calculated in such manner, as may be prescribed, shall be deemed to be the full value
[x=  55.23] [y= 173.94] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] of the consideration received or accruing as a result of such transfer.
[x=  27.62] [y= 185.61] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (4) Every assessee, in the case of a slump sale, shall furnish in the prescribed form a report of an accountant, before the specified date referred to in section 63,
[x=  27.62] [y= 198.34] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] and the report shall—
[x=  40.97] [y= 213.19] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (a) include the computation of the net worth of the undertaking or division; and
[x=  40.87] [y= 225.92] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (b) certify that the net worth has been correctly arrived at as per the provisions of this section.
[x=  27.62] [y= 239.72] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (5) For the purposes of this section,—
[x=  40.97] [y= 254.04] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (a) the "net worth" shall be the "aggregate value of total assets" of the undertaking or division, as reduced by the value of its liabilities as appearing in the
[x=  55.23] [y= 263.58] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] books of account, and for computing net worth, any change in the value of assets due to revaluation shall be ignored;
[x=  40.87] [y= 274.19] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (b) the "aggregate value of total assets" shall,—
[x=  71.95] [y= 286.92] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (i) for depreciable assets, be the written down value of the block of assets determined under section 41(1)(c);
[x=  69.60] [y= 297.53] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (ii) for capital asset being goodwill of a business or profession, which was not acquired by the assessee by purchase from a previous owner, be nil;
[x=  67.24] [y= 308.14] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (iii) for capital assets for which the entire expenditure has been allowed or is allowable as a deduction under section 46, be nil; and
[x=  68.19] [y= 318.75] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (iv) for other assets, be the book value.
