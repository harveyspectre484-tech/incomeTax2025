[x=  27.62] [y=  65.20] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] "Transfer" and "revocable transfer" defined.
[x=  27.62] [y=  80.59] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] 98. For the purposes of sections 96 and 97, and this section,—
[x=  40.80] [y=  95.44] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (a) "transfer" includes any settlement, trust, covenant, agreement or arrangement;
[x=  40.80] [y= 106.05] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (b) a transfer shall be deemed to be revocable, if—
[x=  71.75] [y= 116.66] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (i) it contains any provision for the direct or indirect re-transfer of the whole or any part of the income or assets to the transferor; or
[x=  69.33] [y= 127.26] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (ii) it, in any way, gives the transferor a right to re-assume power directly or indirectly over the whole or any part of the income or assets.
