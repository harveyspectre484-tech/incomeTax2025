[x=  27.62] [y=  65.20] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] Penalty for furnishing incorrect information in reports or certificates.
[x=  27.62] [y=  80.59] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] 463. (1) Any accountant or merchant banker or registered valuer, shall be liable to pay a penalty of Rs. 10000 for any incorrect information in any report or
[x=  27.62] [y=  93.32] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] certificate furnished under any provision of this Act or the rules made thereunder.
[x=  27.62] [y= 109.23] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (2) The penalty under sub-section (1) shall be payable for each such report or certificate.
[x=  27.62] [y= 124.61] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (3) The penalty under sub-section (1) shall be payable on directions of the Assessing Officer or the Joint Commissioner (Appeals) or the Commissioner
[x=  27.62] [y= 137.34] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (Appeals) where the incorrect information mentioned in sub-section (1) is found by such authority in the course of any proceedings under this Act.

[x=  40.80] [y= 167.58] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (a) "merchant banker" means Category I merchant banker registered with the Securities and Exchange Board of India established under section 3 of the
