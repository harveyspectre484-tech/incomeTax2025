[x=  27.62] [y=  80.59] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] 516. The amount of total income computed or any amount payable or refundable under this Act, shall be rounded off to the nearest multiple of Rs. 10
[x=  27.62] [y=  93.32] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] ignoring any part of a rupee consisting of paise and thereafter if such amount is not a multiple of ten, then—
[x=  40.80] [y= 108.17] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (a) such amount shall be increased to the next higher amount which is a multiple of ten, if the last figure in that amount is five or more; or
[x=  40.80] [y= 118.78] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (b) such amount shall be reduced to the next lower amount which is a multiple of ten, if the last figure is less than five,
[x=  27.62] [y= 130.45] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] and the amount so rounded off shall be deemed to be the total income of the assessee or the amount payable or refundable, as the case may be, under this
