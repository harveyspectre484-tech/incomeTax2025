[x=  27.62] [y=  65.20] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] Application of Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 to proceedings before Special Court.
[x=  27.62] [y=  80.59] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] 498. (1) Save as otherwise provided in this Act, the provisions of Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 (46 of 2023) (including the provisions as to
[x=  27.62] [y=  93.32] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] bails or bonds), shall apply to the proceedings before a Special Court and the person conducting the prosecution before the Special Court, shall be deemed
[x=  27.62] [y= 106.05] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] to be a Public Prosecutor.
[x=  27.62] [y= 121.96] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (2) The Central Government may also appoint a Special Public Prosecutor for any case or class or group of cases.
[x=  27.62] [y= 137.34] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (3) A person shall not be qualified to be appointed as a Public Prosecutor or a Special Public Prosecutor under this section unless he has been in practice as
[x=  27.62] [y= 150.07] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] an advocate for not less than seven years, requiring special knowledge of law.
[x=  27.62] [y= 165.46] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (4) Every person appointed as a Public Prosecutor or a Special Public Prosecutor under this section shall be deemed to be a Public Prosecutor within the
[x=  27.62] [y= 178.19] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] meaning of section 2(1)(v) of the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 (46 of 2023), and the provisions of that Sanhita shall have effect accordingly.
