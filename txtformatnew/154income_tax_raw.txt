[x=  27.62] [y=  80.59] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] Deduction in case of a person with disability.
[x=  27.62] [y=  96.50] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] 154. (1) An individual, being resident in India, who is certified by a medical authority, at any time during the tax year, as a person with disability or person
[x=  27.62] [y= 109.23] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] with severe disability, shall be allowed a deduction of Rs. 75000 or Rs. 125000, respectively, while computing his total income.
[x=  27.62] [y= 124.61] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (2) The deduction under sub-section (1) shall be allowed only if all of the following conditions are fulfilled:—
[x=  40.97] [y= 138.93] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (a) the individual furnishes a copy of the certificate issued by the medical authority;
[x=  40.87] [y= 151.66] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (b) if the certificate specifies that the disability needs reassessment of its extent after a period stipulated in it, the deduction shall not be allowed for any tax
[x=  55.23] [y= 161.21] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] year succeeding the tax year in which the certificate expires, unless a new disability certificate is obtained and furnished; and
[x=  41.94] [y= 171.82] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (c) the certificate referred to in clauses (a) and (b) of this sub-section is furnished in the form and manner, as may be prescribed, along with the return of
[x=  55.23] [y= 181.37] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] income under section 263 for the tax year in which the deduction is claimed.
[x=  27.62] [y= 193.04] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (3) For the purposes of this section, "disability", "medical authority", "person with disability" or "person with severe disability" shall have the same meanings
[x=  27.62] [y= 205.77] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] as provided in section 127.
