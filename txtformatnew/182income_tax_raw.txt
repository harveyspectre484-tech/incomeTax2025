[x=  27.62] [y=  65.20] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] Treatment of connected person and accommodating party.
[x=  27.62] [y=  80.59] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] 182. In this Chapter, in determining whether a tax benefit exists,—
[x=  40.80] [y=  95.44] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (a) the parties who are connected persons in relation to each other may be treated as one and the same person;
[x=  40.80] [y= 106.05] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (b) any accommodating party may be disregarded;
[x=  41.27] [y= 116.66] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (c) the accommodating party and any other party may be treated as one and the same person;
[x=  40.80] [y= 127.26] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (d) the arrangement may be considered or looked through by disregarding any corporate structure.
