[x=  27.62] [y=  65.20] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] Submission of statements by producers of cinematograph films or persons engaged in specified activity.
[x=  27.62] [y=  80.59] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] 507. (1) Any person carrying on the production of a cinematograph film or engaged in any specified activity, or both, during the whole or any part of any
[x=  27.62] [y=  93.32] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] tax year shall, furnish within such period, a statement in such form and in such manner, to the prescribed income-tax authority as may be prescribed.
[x=  27.62] [y= 109.23] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (2) The statement referred in sub-section (1) shall contain particulars of all payments of over Rs. 50000 in the aggregate made by him or due from him to
[x=  27.62] [y= 121.96] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] each such person as is engaged by him in such production or specified activity.
[x=  27.62] [y= 137.34] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (3) For the purposes of this section, the expression "specified activity" means any event management, documentary production, production of programmes
[x=  27.62] [y= 150.07] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] for telecasting on television or over the top platforms or any other similar platform, sports event management, other performing arts or any other activity as
[x=  27.62] [y= 162.80] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] the Central Government may, by notification, specify.
