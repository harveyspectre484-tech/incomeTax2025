[x=  27.62] [y=  65.20] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] Special provision for full value of consideration for transfer of share other than quoted share.
[x=  27.62] [y=  80.59] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] 79. (1) If the consideration received or accruing from the transfer of a capital asset, being share of a company other than a quoted share, is less than the fair
[x=  27.62] [y=  93.32] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] market value of such share determined in the manner as may be prescribed, the value so determined shall be deemed to be the full value of consideration
[x=  27.62] [y= 106.05] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] received or accruing as a result of such transfer for the purposes of section 72.
[x=  27.62] [y= 121.96] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (2) The provisions of sub-section (1) shall not apply to any consideration received or accruing as a result of transfer by such class of persons and subject to
[x=  27.62] [y= 134.69] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] such conditions, as may be prescribed.
[x=  27.62] [y= 150.07] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (3) For the purposes of this section, the expression "quoted share" means the share quoted on any recognised stock exchange with regularity from time to time,
[x=  27.62] [y= 162.80] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] where the quotation of such share is based on current transaction made in the ordinary course of business.
