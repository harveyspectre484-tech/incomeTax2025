[x=  27.62] [y=  65.20] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] Impermissible avoidance arrangement.
[x=  27.62] [y=  80.59] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] 179. (1) An impermissible avoidance arrangement means an arrangement, the main purpose of which is to obtain a tax benefit, and it—
[x=  40.80] [y=  95.44] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (a) creates rights, or obligations, which are not ordinarily created between persons dealing at arm's length;
[x=  40.80] [y= 106.05] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (b) results, directly or indirectly, in the misuse, or abuse, of the provisions of this Act;
[x=  41.27] [y= 116.66] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (c) lacks commercial substance or is deemed to lack commercial substance under section 180, in whole or in part; or
[x=  40.80] [y= 127.26] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (d) is entered into, or carried out, by means, or in a manner, which are not ordinarily employed for bona fide purposes.
[x=  27.62] [y= 138.93] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (2) An arrangement shall be presumed, unless it is proved to the contrary by the assessee, to have been entered into, or carried out, for the main purpose of
[x=  27.62] [y= 151.66] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] obtaining a tax benefit, if the main purpose of a step in, or a part of, the arrangement is to obtain a tax benefit, irrespective of the fact that the main purpose
[x=  27.62] [y= 164.39] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] of the whole arrangement is not to obtain a tax benefit.
