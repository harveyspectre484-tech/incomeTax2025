[x=  27.62] [y=  65.20] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] Penalty payable when tax in default.
[x=  27.62] [y=  80.59] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] 412. (1) When an assessee is in default or is deemed to be in default in making a payment of tax, he shall, in addition to the amount of the arrears and the
[x=  27.62] [y=  93.32] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] amount of interest payable under section 411(3), be liable, by way of penalty, to pay—
[x=  40.80] [y= 108.17] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (a) such amount as the Assessing Officer may direct; and
[x=  40.80] [y= 118.78] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (b) in the case of a continuing default, such further amount or amounts as the Assessing Officer may, from time to time, direct.
[x=  27.62] [y= 130.45] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (2) The total amount of penalty under sub-section (1) shall not exceed the amount of tax in arrears.
[x=  27.62] [y= 145.83] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (3) No penalty under sub-section (1) shall be levied—
[x=  40.80] [y= 160.15] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (a) unless the assessee has been given a reasonable opportunity of being heard;
[x=  40.80] [y= 170.76] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (b) where the assessee proves to the satisfaction of the Assessing Officer that the default was for good and sufficient reasons.
[x=  27.62] [y= 182.43] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (4) The assessee shall not cease to be liable to any penalty under sub-section (1) merely by reason of the fact that before the levy of such penalty he has
[x=  27.62] [y= 195.16] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] paid the tax.
[x=  27.62] [y= 211.07] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (5) Where as a result of any final order the amount of tax, with respect to the default in the payment of which the penalty was levied, has been wholly
[x=  27.62] [y= 223.80] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] reduced, the penalty levied shall be cancelled and the amount of penalty paid shall be refunded.
