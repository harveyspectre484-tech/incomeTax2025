[x=  27.62] [y=  65.20] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] Apportionment of income between spouses governed by Portuguese Civil Code.
[x=  27.62] [y=  80.59] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] 10. If a husband and wife are governed by the community of property system (known as "COMMUNIAO DOS BENS" under the Portuguese Civil Code of
[x=  27.62] [y=  93.32] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] 1860) in force in the State of Goa and the Union Territories of Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu, then—
[x=  40.80] [y= 108.17] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (a) their income under any head of income shall not be assessed together as that of such community of property (whether treated as an association of
[x=  55.20] [y= 117.72] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] persons or a body of individuals);
[x=  40.80] [y= 128.33] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (b) the income mentioned in clause (a) under each head of income other than "Salaries" shall be divided equally between the husband and the wife;
[x=  41.27] [y= 138.93] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (c) the income so divided shall be included separately in the total income of the husband and the wife respectively, and the remaining provisions of this
[x=  55.20] [y= 148.48] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] Act shall apply accordingly; and
[x=  40.80] [y= 159.09] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (d) where either the husband or the wife, has any income under the head "Salaries", that income shall be included in the total income of the spouse who
[x=  55.20] [y= 168.64] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] has actually earned it.
