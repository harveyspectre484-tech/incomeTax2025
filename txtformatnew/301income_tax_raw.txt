[x=  40.80] [y=  95.44] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (a) "block period" means the aggregate of—
[x=  71.75] [y= 106.05] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (i) the period comprising six tax years preceding the tax year in which the search was initiated or any requisition was made; and
[x=  69.33] [y= 116.66] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (ii) the period starting from the 1st April of the tax year in which search was initiated or requisition was made and ending on the date of the
[x=  84.28] [y= 126.20] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] execution of the last of the authorisations for such search or such requisition;
[x=  40.80] [y= 136.81] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (b) "requisition" means requisition of books of account, other documents or any assets under section 248;
[x=  41.27] [y= 147.42] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (c) "search" means a search initiated under section 247;
[x=  40.80] [y= 158.03] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (d) "the last of the authorisations" shall be deemed to have been executed,—
[x=  71.75] [y= 168.64] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (i) in the case of search, on the conclusion of search as recorded in the last panchnama drawn in relation to any person in whose case the
[x=  84.28] [y= 178.19] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] warrant of authorisation has been issued, irrespective of whether or not any seizure is recorded in such panchnama;
[x=  69.33] [y= 188.79] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (ii) in the case of requisition, on the actual receipt of the books of account or other documents or assets by the Authorised Officer;
[x=  41.27] [y= 199.40] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (e) "undisclosed income" includes any money, bullion, jewellery, virtual digital asset or other valuable article or thing or any expenditure or any income
[x=  55.20] [y= 208.95] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] based on any entry in the books of account or other documents or transactions, where such money, bullion, jewellery, virtual digital asset, valuable
[x=  55.20] [y= 218.50] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] article, thing, entry in the books of account or other document or transaction represents wholly or partly income or property which has not been or
