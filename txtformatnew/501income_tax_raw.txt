[x=  40.80] [y= 108.17] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (a) by post or by such courier services as may be approved by the Board;
[x=  40.80] [y= 118.78] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (b) in such manner as provided under the Code of Civil Procedure, 1908 (5 of 1908) for the purposes of service of summons;
