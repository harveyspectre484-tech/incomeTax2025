[x=  27.62] [y=  96.50] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] 403. (1) Advance tax shall be payable during any financial year in respect of the current income of the assessee, as per the provisions of this Part.
[x=  27.62] [y= 111.88] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (2) For the purposes of this Part, "current income" of a tax year means the total income of the assessee which would be chargeable to tax for such tax year.
[x=  27.62] [y= 127.26] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (3) The provisions of sub-section (1) shall not apply to an individual resident in India, who—
[x=  40.80] [y= 142.12] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (a) does not have any income chargeable under the head "Profits and gains of business or profession"; and
[x=  40.80] [y= 152.73] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (b) is of the age of sixty years or more at any time during the tax year.
