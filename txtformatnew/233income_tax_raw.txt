[x=  27.62] [y=  93.32] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] provisions relating to the tonnage tax scheme shall, as far as may be, apply to the amalgamated company, if it is a qualifying company.
[x=  27.62] [y= 109.23] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (2) Where the amalgamated company is not a tonnage tax company, it shall exercise an option for tonnage tax scheme under section 231(1) within three

[x=  27.62] [y= 137.34] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (3) Where the amalgamating companies are tonnage tax companies, the provisions of this Part shall, as far as may be, apply to the amalgamated company

[x=  27.62] [y= 165.46] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (4) Where one of the amalgamating companies is a qualifying company as on the 1st October, 2004 and which has not exercised the option for tonnage tax
[x=  27.62] [y= 178.19] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] scheme before the 1st January, 2005, the provisions of this Part shall not apply to the amalgamated company and the income of the amalgamated company
[x=  27.62] [y= 190.92] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] from the business of operating qualifying ships shall be computed as per the other provisions of this Act.
[x=  27.62] [y= 206.83] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (5) Where in a scheme of demerger, the demerged company transfers its business to the resulting company before the expiry of the option for tonnage tax

[x=  27.62] [y= 247.67] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (6) The option for tonnage tax scheme in respect of the demerged company shall remain in force for the unexpired period of the tonnage tax scheme if it
