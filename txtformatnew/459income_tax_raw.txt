[x=  27.62] [y=  65.20] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] Penalty for failure to furnish report or for furnishing inaccurate report under section 511.
[x=  27.62] [y=  80.59] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] 459. (1) If any reporting entity referred to in section 511, required to furnish the report referred to in sub-section (2) of the said section, for a reporting
[x=  27.62] [y=  93.32] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] accounting year, fails to do so, the prescribed authority under that section may impose on such entity, a penalty of—
[x=  40.80] [y= 108.17] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (a) Rs. 5000 for every day for which the failure continues, if the period of failure does not exceed one month;
[x=  40.80] [y= 118.78] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (b) Rs. 15000 for every day for which the failure continues beyond the period of one month.
[x=  27.62] [y= 130.45] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (2) If any reporting entity referred to in section 511 fails to produce the information and documents within the period allowed under sub-section (7) of the
[x=  27.62] [y= 143.18] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] said section, the prescribed authority under that section may impose on such entity, a penalty of Rs. 5000 for every day during which the failure continues,
[x=  27.62] [y= 155.91] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] beginning from the day immediately following the day on which the period for furnishing the information and document expires.
[x=  27.62] [y= 171.29] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (3) If the failure referred to in sub-section (1) or (2) continues after an order imposing a penalty under the said sub-section, has been served on the entity,
[x=  27.62] [y= 184.02] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] then, irrespective of the provisions of the said sub-sections, the prescribed authority may impose penalty of fifty thousand rupees for every day for which
[x=  27.62] [y= 196.75] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] such failure continues beginning from the date of service of such order.
[x=  27.62] [y= 212.13] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (4) If a reporting entity referred to in section 511 provides inaccurate information in the report furnished under sub-section (2) of the said section, the
[x=  27.62] [y= 224.86] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] prescribed authority under that section may impose on such entity, a penalty of Rs. 500000, if—
[x=  40.80] [y= 239.72] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (a) the entity has knowledge of the inaccuracy at the time of furnishing the report but fails to inform the prescribed authority; or
[x=  40.80] [y= 250.32] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (b) the entity discovers the inaccuracy after the report is furnished and fails to inform the prescribed authority and furnish correct report within fifteen
[x=  55.20] [y= 259.87] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] days of such discovery; or
[x=  41.27] [y= 270.48] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (c) the entity furnishes inaccurate information or document in response to the notice issued under section 511(7).
