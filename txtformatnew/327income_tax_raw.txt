[x= 201.03] [y=  65.20] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] 15.—Change in constitution, succession and dissolution
[x=  27.62] [y=  80.59] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] Change in constitution of a firm.
[x=  27.62] [y=  96.50] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] 327. (1) Where at the time of making an assessment under section 270 or 271, it is found that a change has occurred in the constitution of a firm, the
[x=  27.62] [y= 109.23] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] assessment shall be made on the firm as constituted at the time of making the assessment.
[x=  27.62] [y= 124.61] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (2) For the purposes of this section, there is a change in the constitution of the firm—
[x=  40.80] [y= 138.93] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (a) if one or more of the partners cease to be partners or one or more new partners are admitted, subject to the condition that at least one person who
[x=  55.20] [y= 148.48] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] was partner of the firm before the change continues as partner after such change; or
[x=  40.80] [y= 159.09] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (b) where all the partners continue with a change in their respective shares or in the shares of some of them.
[x=  27.62] [y= 170.76] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (3) The provisions of sub-section (2)(a) shall not apply to a case where the firm is dissolved on the death of any of its partners.
