[x=  27.62] [y=  80.59] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] Salaries.
[x=  27.62] [y=  96.50] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] 15. (1) The following income shall be chargeable to income-tax under the head "Salaries":—
[x=  40.80] [y= 110.82] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (a) any salary due from an employer to an assessee in the tax year, whether paid or not;
[x=  40.80] [y= 121.43] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (b) any salary paid or allowed to him in the tax year by or on behalf of an employer though not due or before it became due to him;
[x=  41.27] [y= 132.04] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (c) any arrears of salary paid or allowed to him in the tax year by or on behalf of an employer, if not charged to income-tax for any earlier tax year.
[x=  27.62] [y= 143.71] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (2) For the purposes of sub-section (1), employer includes former employer.
[x=  27.62] [y= 159.09] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (3) If any salary paid in advance is included in the total income of any person for any tax year, it shall not be included again in the total income of such
[x=  27.62] [y= 171.82] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] person when the salary becomes due.
[x=  27.62] [y= 187.73] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (4) Any salary, bonus, commission or remuneration, by whatever name called, due to, or received by, a partner of a firm from the firm shall not be regarded
[x=  27.62] [y= 200.46] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] as salary for the purposes of this section.
