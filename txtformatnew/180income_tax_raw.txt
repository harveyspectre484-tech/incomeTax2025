[x=  27.62] [y=  65.20] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] Arrangement to lack commercial substance.
[x=  27.62] [y=  80.59] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] 180. (1) An arrangement shall be deemed to lack commercial substance, if—
[x=  40.80] [y=  95.44] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (a) the substance or effect of the arrangement as a whole, is inconsistent with, or differs significantly from, the form of its individual steps or a part; or
[x=  40.80] [y= 106.05] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (b) it involves or includes—
[x=  71.75] [y= 116.66] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (i) round trip financing;
[x=  69.33] [y= 127.26] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (ii) an accommodating party;
[x=  66.91] [y= 137.87] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (iii) elements that have effect of offsetting or cancelling each other;
[x=  67.93] [y= 148.48] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (iv) a transaction which is conducted through one or more persons and disguises the value, location, source, ownership or control of funds
[x=  84.28] [y= 158.03] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] which is the subject matter of such transaction;
[x=  41.27] [y= 168.64] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (c) it involves the location of an asset or of a transaction or of the place of residence of any party which is without any substantial commercial purpose
[x=  55.20] [y= 178.19] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] other than obtaining a tax benefit (but for the provisions of this Chapter) for a party; or
[x=  40.80] [y= 188.79] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (d) it does not have a significant effect upon the business risks or net cash flows of any party to the arrangement apart from any effect attributable to the
[x=  55.20] [y= 198.34] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] tax benefit that would be obtained (but for the provisions of this Chapter).
[x=  27.62] [y= 210.01] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (2) In sub-section (1), round trip financing includes any arrangement in which, through a series of transactions—
[x=  40.80] [y= 224.33] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (a) funds are transferred among the parties to the arrangement; and
[x=  40.80] [y= 234.94] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (b) such transactions do not have any substantial commercial purpose other than obtaining the tax benefit (but for the provisions of this Chapter),
[x=  27.62] [y= 246.61] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] without having any regard to—
[x=  39.85] [y= 260.93] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (A) whether or not the funds involved in the round trip financing can be traced to any funds transferred to, or received by, any party in connection with
[x=  55.20] [y= 270.48] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] the arrangement;
[x=  39.85] [y= 281.09] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (B) the time, or sequence, in which the funds involved in the round trip financing are transferred or received; or
[x=  39.38] [y= 291.70] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (C) the means by, or manner in, or mode through, which funds involved in the round trip financing are transferred or received.
[x=  27.62] [y= 303.37] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (3) The following may be relevant but shall not be sufficient for determining whether an arrangement lacks commercial substance or not:—
[x=  40.80] [y= 318.22] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (a) the period or time for which the arrangement (including operations therein) exists;
[x=  40.80] [y= 328.83] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (b) the fact of payment of taxes, directly or indirectly, under the arrangement;
[x=  41.27] [y= 339.44] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (c) the fact that an exit route (including transfer of any activity or business or operations) is provided by the arrangement.
