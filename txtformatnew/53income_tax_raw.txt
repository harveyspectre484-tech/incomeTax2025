[x=  27.62] [y=  80.59] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] 53. (1) In case of transfer of an asset (other than a capital asset), being land or building or both, if the consideration received or accrued from such transfer
[x=  27.62] [y=  93.32] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] is less than the stamp duty value, then such stamp duty value for computing profits and gains from transfer of such asset shall be deemed to be the full value
[x=  27.62] [y= 106.05] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] of consideration.
[x=  27.62] [y= 121.96] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (2) The provisions of sub-section (1) shall not apply if the stamp duty value does not exceed 110% of the consideration received or accrued and in such a
[x=  27.62] [y= 134.69] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] case, the consideration received or accrued shall be deemed to be the full value of consideration.
[x=  27.62] [y= 150.07] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (3) If the date of agreement fixing the value of consideration for transfer of asset and date of registration for transfer of such asset are different, then the
[x=  27.62] [y= 162.80] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] stamp duty value as on date of agreement may be taken to be the full value of consideration under sub-section (1).
[x=  27.62] [y= 178.19] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (4) The provisions of sub-section (3) shall apply only in a case where the amount of consideration or a part thereof has been received by specified banking
[x=  27.62] [y= 190.92] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] or online mode on or before the date of agreement for transfer of such asset.
[x=  27.62] [y= 206.83] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (5) For the determination of the stamp duty value under sub-section (1), the provisions of section 78(2) and (3)shall apply.
