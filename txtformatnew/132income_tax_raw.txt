[x=  27.62] [y=  65.20] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] Deduction in respect of purchase of electric vehicle.
[x=  27.62] [y=  80.59] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] 132. (1) An assessee, being an individual, shall be allowed a deduction of interest payable on loan taken by him from any financial institution for the
[x=  27.62] [y=  93.32] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] purpose of purchase of an electric vehicle, as per the provisions of this section.
[x=  27.62] [y= 109.23] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (2) The deduction under sub-section (1) shall be subject to the condition that the loan has been sanctioned by the financial institution during the period
[x=  27.62] [y= 121.96] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] beginning on the 1st April, 2019 and ending on the 31st March, 2023.
[x=  27.62] [y= 137.34] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (3) The deduction under sub-section (1) shall not exceed Rs. 150000 and shall be allowed in computing the total income of the individual for the tax year
[x=  27.62] [y= 150.07] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] beginning on the 1st April, 2019 and subsequent tax years.
[x=  27.62] [y= 165.46] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (4) Where a deduction under this section is allowed for any interest referred to in sub-section (1), deduction shall not be allowed in respect of such interest
[x=  27.62] [y= 178.19] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] under any other provision of this Act for the same or any other tax year.
[x=  27.62] [y= 194.10] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (5) For the purposes of this section,—
[x=  40.80] [y= 208.42] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (a) "electric vehicle" means a vehicle powered exclusively by an electric motor, whose traction energy is supplied exclusively by traction battery
[x=  55.20] [y= 217.97] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] installed in the vehicle and has such electric regenerative braking system, which during braking provides for the conversion of vehicle kinetic
[x=  55.20] [y= 227.52] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] energy into electrical energy;
[x=  40.80] [y= 238.12] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (b) "financial institution" means a banking company to which the Banking Regulation Act, 1949 (10 of 1949) applies, or any bank or banking institution
[x=  55.20] [y= 247.67] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] referred to in section 51 of that Act and includes a non-banking financial company.
