[x=  27.62] [y=  65.20] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] Set off of losses under any other head of income.
[x=  27.62] [y=  80.59] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] 109. (1) Subject to the provisions of this Chapter, for any tax year, if income computed under any head of income (other than "Capital gains") is a loss, such
[x=  27.62] [y=  93.32] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] loss shall be set off against income of the assessee under any other head, including "Capital gains", if any, assessable for that tax year, subject to the
[x=  27.62] [y= 106.05] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] following conditions:—
[x=  40.80] [y= 120.90] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (a) loss under the head "Profits and gains of business or profession" shall not be set off against income assessable under the head "Salaries"; and
[x=  40.80] [y= 131.51] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (b) loss under the head "Income from house property" shall be set off to the extent of Rs. 200000 against income under any other head.
[x=  27.62] [y= 143.18] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (2) For any tax year, the loss under the head "Capital gains" shall not be set off against income under any other head.
