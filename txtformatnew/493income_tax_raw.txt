[x=  27.62] [y=  93.32] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] prosecution of any person for an offence under this Chapter, and all such entries may be proved either by—
[x=  40.80] [y= 108.17] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (a) the production of the records or other documents in the custody of the income-tax authority containing such entries; or
[x=  40.80] [y= 118.78] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (b) the production of a copy of the entries certified by the income-tax authority having custody of the records or other documents under its signature
[x=  55.20] [y= 128.33] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] and stating that it is a true copy of the original entries and that such original entries are contained in the records or other documents in its custody.
