[x=  27.62] [y=  65.20] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] Procedure of Appellate Tribunal.
[x=  27.62] [y=  80.59] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] 364. (1) The powers and functions of the Appellate Tribunal may be exercised and discharged by Benches constituted by the President of the Appellate
[x=  27.62] [y=  93.32] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] Tribunal from among the members thereof.
[x=  27.62] [y= 109.23] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (2) Subject to the provisions contained in sub-section (3), a Bench shall consist of one Judicial Member and one accountant member.
[x=  27.62] [y= 124.61] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (3) The President, or any other member of the Appellate Tribunal authorised in this behalf by the Central Government, may sitting singly, dispose of any
[x=  27.62] [y= 137.34] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] case allotted to the Bench, pertaining to an assessee whose total income as computed by the Assessing Officer in the case does not exceed rupees fifty lakh.
[x=  27.62] [y= 152.73] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (4) The President of the Appellate Tribunal may, for the disposal of any particular case, constitute a Special Bench consisting of three or more members,
[x=  27.62] [y= 165.46] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] one of whom shall necessarily be a judicial member and one an accountant member.
[x=  27.62] [y= 181.37] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (5) If the members of a Bench differ in opinion on any point, the point shall be decided according to the opinion of the majority, if there is a majority, but if
[x=  27.62] [y= 194.10] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] the members are equally divided, they shall state the point or points on which they differ, and the case shall be referred by the President of the Appellate
[x=  27.62] [y= 206.83] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] Tribunal for hearing on such point or points by one or more of the other members of the Appellate Tribunal, and such point or points shall be decided
[x=  27.62] [y= 219.56] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] according to the opinion of the majority of the members of the Appellate Tribunal who have heard the case, including those who first heard it.
[x=  27.62] [y= 234.94] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (6) Subject to the provisions of this Act, the Appellate Tribunal shall have power to regulate its own procedure and the procedure of Benches thereof in all
[x=  27.62] [y= 247.67] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] matters arising out of the exercise of its powers or of the discharge of its functions, including the places at which the Benches shall hold their sittings.
[x=  27.62] [y= 263.05] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (7) The Appellate Tribunal, for the purposes of discharging its functions, shall have all the powers which are vested in the income-tax authorities referred to
[x=  27.62] [y= 275.78] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] in section 246, and any proceeding before the Appellate Tribunal shall be deemed to be a judicial proceeding within the meaning of sections 229 and 267
[x=  27.62] [y= 288.51] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] and for the purposes of section 233 of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 (45 of 2023), and the Appellate Tribunal shall be deemed to be a Civil Court for
[x=  27.62] [y= 301.24] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] all the purposes of section 215 and Chapter XXXVII of the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 (46 of 2023).
