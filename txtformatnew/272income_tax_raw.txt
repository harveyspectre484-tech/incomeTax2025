[x=  27.62] [y=  65.20] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] Power of Joint Commissioner to issue directions in certain cases.
[x=  27.62] [y=  80.59] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] 272. (1) A Joint Commissioner may, on his own motion or on a reference being made to him by the Assessing Officer or on the application of an assessee,
[x=  27.62] [y=  93.32] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] call for and examine the record of any proceeding in which an assessment is pending and, if he considers that, having regard to the nature of the case or the
[x=  27.62] [y= 106.05] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] amount involved or for any other reason, it is necessary or expedient so to do, he may—
[x=  40.80] [y= 120.90] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (a) issue such directions as he thinks fit for the guidance of the Assessing Officer to enable him to complete the assessment; and
[x=  40.80] [y= 131.51] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (b) such directions shall be binding on the Assessing Officer.
[x=  27.62] [y= 143.18] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (2) No directions which are prejudicial to the assessee shall be issued under sub-section (1) without giving an opportunity of being heard to the assessee.
[x=  27.62] [y= 158.56] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (3) For the purposes of this section, no direction as to the lines on which an investigation connected with the assessment should be made, shall be deemed
[x=  27.62] [y= 171.29] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] to be a direction prejudicial to the assessee.
