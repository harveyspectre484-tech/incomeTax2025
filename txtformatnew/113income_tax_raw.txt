[x=  27.62] [y=  65.20] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] Set off and carry forward of losses computed in respect of speculation business.
[x=  27.62] [y=  80.59] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] 113. (1) Any loss, computed in respect of a speculation business carried on by the assessee shall be set off only against profits and gains of another
[x=  27.62] [y=  93.32] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] speculation business.
[x=  27.62] [y= 109.23] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (2) Where for any tax year, loss computed in respect of a speculation business cannot be wholly set off under sub-section (1), so much of the loss not so set
[x=  27.62] [y= 121.96] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] off or the whole loss, as the case may be, shall be carried forward to the following tax year and—
[x=  42.68] [y= 136.28] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (i) be set off against the profits and gains, if any, of any speculation business carried on by him for such tax year; and
[x=  40.26] [y= 146.89] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (ii) if the loss cannot be wholly so set off, the amount of loss not so set off shall be carried forward to the following tax year and so on.
[x=  27.62] [y= 158.56] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (3) No loss shall be carried forward under this section for more than four tax years immediately succeeding the tax year for which the loss was first
[x=  27.62] [y= 171.29] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] computed.
[x=  27.62] [y= 186.67] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (4) Where any allowance of part thereof under section 33(11) or 45(7) related to the speculation business is to be carried forward, effect shall first be given
[x=  27.62] [y= 199.40] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] to the provision of this section.
[x=  27.62] [y= 215.32] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (5) In this section, where any part of the business of the assessee (being a company) consists of purchase and sale of shares of other companies, then the
[x=  27.62] [y= 228.05] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] assessee shall be deemed to be carrying on a speculation business, to the extent to which its business consists of purchase and sale of such shares.
[x=  27.62] [y= 243.43] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (6) The provisions of sub-section (5) shall not apply to an assessee, being a company, if—
[x=  40.80] [y= 257.75] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (a) its gross total income consists mainly of income which is chargeable under the heads "Income from house property", "Capital gains" or "Income
