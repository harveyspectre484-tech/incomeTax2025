[x=  27.62] [y=  65.20] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] Definitions of certain terms relevant to determination of arm's length price, etc.
[x=  27.62] [y=  80.59] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] 173. For the purposes of this section and sections 161, 162, 163, 165, 171 and 172, unless the context otherwise requires,—
[x=  40.80] [y=  95.44] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (a) "arm's length price" means a price which is applied or proposed to be applied in a transaction between persons other than associated enterprises, in
[x=  55.20] [y= 104.99] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] uncontrolled conditions;
[x=  40.80] [y= 115.60] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (b) "enterprise" means a person (including a permanent establishment of such person) who is, or has been, or is proposed to be, engaged in any activity
[x=  55.20] [y= 125.14] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] relating to—
[x=  71.75] [y= 135.75] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (i) the production, storage, supply, distribution, acquisition or control of articles or goods; or
[x=  69.33] [y= 146.36] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (ii) know-how, patents, copyrights, trade-marks, licences, franchises or any other business or commercial rights of similar nature; or
[x=  66.91] [y= 156.97] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (iii) any data, documentation, drawing or specification relating to any patent, invention, model, design, secret formula or process of which the
[x=  84.28] [y= 166.52] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] other enterprise is the owner or in respect of which the other enterprise has exclusive rights; or
[x=  67.93] [y= 177.12] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (iv) provision of services of any kind; or
[x=  70.35] [y= 187.73] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (v) carrying out any work in pursuance of a contract; or
[x=  67.93] [y= 198.34] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (vi) investment or providing loan; or
[x=  65.51] [y= 208.95] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (vii) business of acquiring, holding, underwriting or dealing with shares, debentures or other securities of any other body corporate,
[x=  51.96] [y= 219.56] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] whether such activity or business is carried on, directly or through one or more of its units or divisions or subsidiaries, or whether such unit or
[x=  55.20] [y= 229.11] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] division or subsidiary is located at the same place where the enterprise is located or at a different place or places;
[x=  41.27] [y= 239.72] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (c) "permanent establishment", referred to in clause (b), includes a fixed place of business through which the business of the enterprise is wholly or
[x=  55.20] [y= 249.26] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] partly carried on;
[x=  40.80] [y= 259.87] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (d) "specified date" means the date one month before the due date for furnishing the return of income under section 263(1) for the relevant tax year;
[x=  41.27] [y= 270.48] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (e) "transaction" includes an arrangement, understanding or action in concert,—
[x=  71.75] [y= 281.09] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (i) whether or not such arrangement, understanding or action is formal or in writing; or
[x=  69.33] [y= 291.70] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (ii) whether or not such arrangement, understanding or action is intended to be enforceable by legal proceeding.
