[x=  27.62] [y=  80.59] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] 366. (1) When an appeal has been filed before the High Court under section 365, it shall be heard by a bench of not less than two Judges of the High Court,
[x=  27.62] [y=  93.32] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] and shall be decided as per the opinion of such Judges or of the majority, if any, of such Judges.
[x=  27.62] [y= 109.23] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (2) Where there is no such majority, the Judges shall state the point of law upon which they differ and the case shall then be heard upon that point only by
[x=  27.62] [y= 121.96] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] one or more of the other Judges of the High Court and such point shall be decided according to the opinion of the majority of the Judges who have heard
[x=  27.62] [y= 134.69] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] the case including those who first heard it.
