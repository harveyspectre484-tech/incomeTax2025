[x=  27.62] [y=  80.59] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] 254. (1) Irrespective of anything contained in any other provision of this Act, an income-tax authority may, for the purposes of collecting any information
[x=  27.62] [y=  93.32] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] which may be useful for, or relevant to, the purposes of this Act, enter––
[x=  40.80] [y= 108.17] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (a) any building or place within the limits of the area assigned to such authority; or
[x=  40.80] [y= 118.78] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (b) any building or place occupied by any person in respect of whom such authority exercises jurisdiction,
[x=  27.62] [y= 130.45] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] at which a business or profession is carried on, regardless of the fact that such place be the principal place or not of such business or profession and require
[x=  27.62] [y= 143.18] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] any proprietor or employee or any other person, who may at that time and place, be attending in any manner to, or helping in, or carrying on of such
[x=  27.62] [y= 155.91] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] business or profession, to furnish such information as may be prescribed.
[x=  27.62] [y= 171.29] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (2) The income-tax authority may enter any place of business or profession referred to in sub-section (1) only during the hours at which such place is open
[x=  27.62] [y= 184.02] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] for the conduct of business or profession.
[x=  27.62] [y= 199.40] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (3) The income-tax authority acting under this section shall, on no account, remove or cause to be removed from the building or place wherein it has
[x=  27.62] [y= 212.13] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] entered, any books of account or other documents or any cash or stock or other valuable article or thing.

[x=  40.80] [y= 252.98] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (b) an Inspector of Income-tax, authorised by the Assessing Officer to exercise the powers conferred under this section in relation to the area in respect
