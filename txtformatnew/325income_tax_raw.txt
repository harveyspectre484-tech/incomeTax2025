[x=  27.62] [y=  65.20] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] Assessment as a firm.
[x=  27.62] [y=  80.59] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] 325. (1) A firm shall be assessed as a firm for the purposes of this Act, if—
[x=  40.80] [y=  95.44] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (a) the partnership is evidenced by an instrument; and
[x=  40.80] [y= 106.05] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (b) the individual shares of the partners are specified in that instrument.
[x=  27.62] [y= 117.72] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (2) A certified copy of the instrument of partnership referred to in sub-section (1) shall accompany the return of income of the firm of the tax year in respect
[x=  27.62] [y= 130.45] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] of which assessment as a firm is first sought.
[x=  27.62] [y= 145.83] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (3) For the purposes of sub-section (2), the copy of the instrument of partnership shall be certified in writing by all the partners (not being minors) or, where
[x=  27.62] [y= 158.56] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] the return is made after the dissolution of the firm, by all persons (not being minors), who were partners in the firm immediately before its dissolution and
[x=  27.62] [y= 171.29] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] by the legal representative of any such partner who is deceased.
[x=  27.62] [y= 186.67] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (4) Where a firm is assessed as such for any tax year, it shall be assessed in the same capacity for every subsequent year, if there is no change in the
[x=  27.62] [y= 199.40] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] constitution of the firm or the shares of the partners as evidenced by the instrument of partnership on the basis of which the assessment as a firm was first
[x=  27.62] [y= 212.13] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] sought.
[x=  27.62] [y= 228.05] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (5) Where any such change had taken place in the tax year, the firm shall furnish a certified copy of the revised instrument of partnership along with the
[x=  27.62] [y= 240.78] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] return of income for such tax year, and all the provisions of this section shall apply accordingly.
[x=  27.62] [y= 256.16] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (6) Irrespective of anything contained in any other provision of this Act, where, in respect of any tax year, there is on the part of a firm any such failure as is
[x=  27.62] [y= 268.89] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] mentioned in section 271,—
[x=  40.80] [y= 283.21] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (a) the firm shall be so assessed that no deduction by way of any payment of interest, salary, bonus, commission or remuneration, by whatever name
[x=  55.20] [y= 292.76] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] called, made by such firm to any partner of such firm shall be allowed in computing the income chargeable under the head "Profits and gains of
[x=  55.20] [y= 302.31] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] business or profession"; and
[x=  40.80] [y= 312.91] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (b) such payment shall not be chargeable to income-tax under section 26(2)(g).
