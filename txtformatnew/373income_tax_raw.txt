[x=  27.62] [y=  65.20] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] Filing of appeal by income-tax authority.
[x=  27.62] [y=  80.59] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] 373. (1) The Board may, from time to time, issue orders, instructions or directions to other income-tax authorities, fixing such monetary limits as it may
[x=  27.62] [y=  93.32] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] deem fit, for the purpose of regulating filing of appeal by any income-tax authority under the provisions of this Chapter.
[x=  27.62] [y= 109.23] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (2) Where, in pursuance of the orders, instructions or directions issued under sub-section (1), an income-tax authority has not filed any appeal on any issue
[x=  27.62] [y= 121.96] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] in the case of an assessee for any tax year, it shall not preclude such authority from filing an appeal on the same issue in the case of—
[x=  40.80] [y= 136.28] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (a) the same assessee for any other tax year; or
[x=  40.80] [y= 146.89] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (b) any other assessee for the same or any other tax year.
[x=  27.62] [y= 158.56] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (3) Where no appeal has been filed by an income-tax authority pursuant to the orders or instructions or directions issued under sub-section (1), it shall not
[x=  27.62] [y= 171.29] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] be lawful for an assessee, being a party in any appeal, to contend that the income-tax authority has acquiesced in the decision on the disputed issue by not
[x=  27.62] [y= 184.02] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] filing an appeal in any case.
[x=  27.62] [y= 199.40] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (4) The Appellate Tribunal or Court, hearing such appeal, shall have regard to the orders, instructions or directions issued under sub-section (1) and the
[x=  27.62] [y= 212.13] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] circumstances under which such appeal was filed or not filed in respect of any case.
