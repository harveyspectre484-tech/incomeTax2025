[x=  27.62] [y=  65.20] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] Special provision for computation of capital gains in case of Market Linked Debenture.
[x=  27.62] [y=  80.59] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] 76. (1) Irrespective of anything contained in section 2(101) or section 72, the gains on the transfer or redemption or maturity, of a capital asset as mentioned
[x=  27.62] [y=  93.32] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] in sub-section (2) shall be treated as short-term capital gains and shall be computed as per sub-section (3).
[x=  27.62] [y= 109.23] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (2) For the purposes of sub-section (1), the capital asset shall be—
[x=  40.80] [y= 123.55] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (a) a unit of a Specified Mutual Fund acquired on or after the 1st April, 2023 or a Market Linked Debenture; or
[x=  40.80] [y= 134.16] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (b) an unlisted bond or an unlisted debenture which is transferred or redeemed or matures on or after the 23rd July, 2024.
[x=  27.62] [y= 145.83] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (3) For the purposes of sub-section (1), the short-term capital gains shall be computed as per the following formula:—
[x=  41.54] [y= 164.39] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] X = A – B – C,
[x=  41.54] [y= 182.43] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] where,—
[x=  81.03] [y= 199.93] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] X = short-term capital gains;
[x=  81.03] [y= 210.54] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] A = full value of consideration received or accruing as a result of the transfer or redemption or maturity of the debenture or unit or bond;
[x=  81.03] [y= 221.15] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] B = the cost of acquisition of the debenture or unit or bond; and
[x=  81.03] [y= 231.76] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] C = the expenditure incurred wholly and exclusively in connection with such transfer or redemption or maturity.
[x=  27.62] [y= 243.43] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (4) In computing capital gains under sub-section (3), no deduction shall be allowed for any sum paid as securities transaction tax as per Chapter VII of the
[x=  27.62] [y= 256.16] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] Finance (No. 2) Act, 2004 (23 of 2004).
[x=  27.62] [y= 271.54] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (5) For the purposes of this section,—
[x=  40.80] [y= 286.39] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (a) "Market Linked Debenture" means a security, by whatever name called, which has an underlying principal component in the form of a debt security
[x=  55.20] [y= 295.94] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] and where the returns are linked to market returns on other underlying securities or indices, and include any security classified or regulated as a

[x=  40.80] [y= 316.10] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (b) "Specified Mutual Fund" means a Mutual Fund, by whatever name called, which invests more than 65% of its total proceeds in debt and money
[x=  55.20] [y= 325.64] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] market instruments or a fund which invests 65% or more of its total proceeds in units of such Mutual Fund, subject to the following:—
[x=  71.75] [y= 336.25] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (i) the percentage of investment in debt and money market instruments or in units of a fund shall be computed with reference to the annual

[x=  69.33] [y= 356.41] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (ii) "debt and money market instruments" shall include any securities, by whatever name called, classified or regulated as debt and money
