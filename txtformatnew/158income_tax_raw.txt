[x=  27.62] [y=  65.20] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] Relief from taxation in income from retirement benefit account maintained in a notified country.
[x=  27.62] [y=  80.59] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] 158. (1) The income accrued to a specified person in a specified account shall be taxed in such manner and in such tax year, as may be prescribed.
[x=  27.62] [y=  96.50] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (2) For the purposes of this section,—
[x=  40.80] [y= 110.82] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (a) "notified country" means a country as may be notified by the Central Government;
[x=  40.80] [y= 121.43] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (b) "specified account" means an account maintained in a notified country by the specified person for his retirement benefits, the income from which is
[x=  55.20] [y= 130.98] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] taxed by that notified country at the time of withdrawal or redemption and, not on accrual basis;
[x=  41.27] [y= 141.59] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (c) "specified person" means a person resident in India having opened a specified account in a notified country while being non-resident in India and
[x=  55.20] [y= 151.13] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] resident in that country.
