[x=  27.62] [y=  65.20] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] Deduction in respect of medical treatment, etc.
[x=  27.62] [y=  80.59] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] 128. (1) An assessee who is resident in India, shall be allowed a deduction of the amount actually paid during the tax year or a sum of Rs. 40000, whichever
[x=  27.62] [y=  93.32] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] is less, from income chargeable to tax of that tax year, for the medical treatment of such disease or ailment as may be prescribed—
[x=  40.80] [y= 108.17] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (a) for himself or a dependant, in case the assessee is an individual; or
[x=  40.80] [y= 118.78] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (b) for any member of a Hindu undivided family, in case the assessee is a Hindu undivided family.
[x=  27.62] [y= 130.45] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (2) A deduction shall be allowed under this section only if the assessee obtains the prescription for the medical treatment from a neurologist, oncologist,
[x=  27.62] [y= 143.18] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] urologist, haematologist, immunologist, or any other specialist, as may be prescribed.
[x=  27.62] [y= 158.56] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (3) The deduction under this section shall be reduced by any amount received under an insurance from an insurer, or reimbursed by an employer, for the
[x=  27.62] [y= 171.29] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] medical treatment of the person as referred to in sub-section (1)(a) or (b).
[x=  27.62] [y= 186.67] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (4) If the amount actually paid is in respect of the assessee or his dependant or any member of a Hindu undivided family of the assessee and who is senior
[x=  27.62] [y= 199.40] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] citizen, the amount of deduction as referred to in sub-section (1) shall be substituted with "Rs. 100000" for "Rs. 40000".
[x=  27.62] [y= 215.32] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (5) For the purposes of this section,—
[x=  40.80] [y= 229.64] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (a) "dependant" shall have the meaning as assigned to it in section 127(9);
[x=  40.80] [y= 240.25] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (b) "insurer" shall have the meaning assigned to it in section 2(9) of the Insurance Act, 1938 (4 of 1938).
