[x=  27.62] [y=  65.20] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] Deduction in respect of interest on loan taken for certain house property.
[x=  27.62] [y=  80.59] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] 131. (1) An assessee, being an individual not eligible to claim deduction under section 130, shall be allowed a deduction of interest payable on loan taken
[x=  27.62] [y=  93.32] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] by him from any financial institution for the purpose of acquisition of a residential house property, subject to a maximum limit of Rs. 150000 in a tax year
[x=  27.62] [y= 106.05] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] and on fulfilment of conditions specified in sub-section (2), for the tax year beginning on the 1st April, 2019 and subsequent tax years.
[x=  27.62] [y= 121.96] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (2) The conditions referred in sub-section (1) shall be the following:—
[x=  40.80] [y= 136.28] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (a) the loan has been sanctioned by the financial institution during the period beginning on the 1st April, 2019 and ending on the 31st March, 2022;
[x=  40.80] [y= 146.89] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (b) the stamp duty value of residential house property does not exceed forty-five lakh rupees; and
[x=  41.27] [y= 157.50] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (c) the assessee does not own any residential house property on the date of sanction of loan.
[x=  27.62] [y= 169.17] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (3) Where a deduction under this section is allowed for any interest referred to in sub-section (1), deduction shall not be allowed in respect of such interest
[x=  27.62] [y= 181.90] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] under any other provision of this Act for the same or any other tax year.
[x=  27.62] [y= 197.28] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (4) For the purposes of this section, the expression "financial institution" shall have the meaning assigned to it in section 130(5)(a).
