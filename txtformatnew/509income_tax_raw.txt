[x=  27.62] [y=  65.20] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] Obligation to furnish information on transaction of crypto-asset.
[x=  27.62] [y=  80.59] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] 509. (1) Any person, being a reporting entity, as may be prescribed, in respect of a crypto-asset, shall furnish information in respect of a transaction of such
[x=  27.62] [y=  93.32] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] crypto-asset in a statement, for such period, within such time, in such form and manner and to such income-tax authority, as may be prescribed.
[x=  27.62] [y= 109.23] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (2) Where the prescribed income-tax authority considers that the statement furnished under sub-section (1) is defective, he may intimate the defect to the
[x=  27.62] [y= 121.96] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] person who has furnished such statement and give him an opportunity of rectifying the defect within thirty days from the date of such intimation or such
[x=  27.62] [y= 134.69] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] further period as may be allowed, and if the defect is not rectified within such period, the provisions of this Act shall apply as if such person had furnished
[x=  27.62] [y= 147.42] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] inaccurate information in the statement.
[x=  27.62] [y= 162.80] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (3) Where a person who is required to furnish a statement under sub-section (1) has not furnished the same within the specified time, the prescribed income-
[x=  27.62] [y= 175.53] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] tax authority may serve upon such person a notice requiring him to furnish such statement within a period not exceeding thirty days from the date of service
[x=  27.62] [y= 188.26] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] of such notice and he shall furnish the statement within the time specified in the notice.
[x=  27.62] [y= 203.65] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (4) If any person, having furnished a statement under sub-section (1), or in pursuance of a notice issued under sub-section (3), comes to know or discovers
[x=  27.62] [y= 216.38] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] any inaccuracy in the information provided in the statement, he shall within ten days inform the prescribed income-tax authority, the inaccuracy in such
[x=  27.62] [y= 229.11] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] statement and furnish the correct information in such manner as may be prescribed.
[x=  27.62] [y= 245.02] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (5) The Central Government may, by rules prescribe—
[x=  40.80] [y= 259.34] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (a) the persons referred to in sub-section (1) to be registered with the prescribed income-tax authority;
[x=  40.80] [y= 269.95] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (b) the nature of information and the manner in which such information shall be maintained by the persons referred to in clause (a); and
[x=  41.27] [y= 280.56] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (c) the due diligence to be carried out by the persons referred to in sub-section (1) for the purpose of identification of any crypto-asset user or owner.
[x=  27.62] [y= 292.23] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (6) For the purposes of this section, the expression "crypto-asset" shall have the meaning assigned to it in section 2(111)(d).
