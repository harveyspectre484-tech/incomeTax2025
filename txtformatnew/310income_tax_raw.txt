[x=  27.62] [y=  80.59] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] 310. (1) Income-tax shall not be payable by an assessee (who is a member of an association of persons or body of individuals) in respect of his share in the
[x=  27.62] [y=  93.32] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] income of the association of persons or body of individuals computed in the manner provided in section 309, except in a case referred to in sub-section (2).
[x=  27.62] [y= 109.23] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (2) Where no income-tax is chargeable on the total income of the association of persons or body of individuals, the share of a member computed as
[x=  27.62] [y= 121.96] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] provided in section 309 shall be chargeable to tax as part of his total income.
[x=  27.62] [y= 137.34] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (3) Where no income-tax is payable by an assessee under sub-section (1),—
[x=  40.80] [y= 151.66] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (a) if the association of persons or body of individuals is chargeable to tax on its total income at the maximum marginal rate or any higher rate under
[x=  55.20] [y= 161.21] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] any of the provisions of this Act, the share of a member computed as aforesaid shall not be included in his total income;
[x=  40.80] [y= 171.82] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (b) in any other case, the share of a member computed as aforesaid shall form part of his total income.
