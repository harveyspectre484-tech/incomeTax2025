[x=  27.62] [y=  80.59] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] 360. (1) In disposing of an appeal, the Commissioner (Appeals) or the Joint Commissioner (Appeals), shall have the following powers:—
[x=  40.80] [y=  95.44] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (a) in an appeal against an order of assessment, he may confirm, reduce, enhance or annul the assessment;
[x=  40.80] [y= 106.05] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (b) where such appeal is against an order of assessment made under section 271, the Commissioner (Appeals) may set aside the assessment and refer
[x=  55.20] [y= 115.60] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] the case back to the Assessing Officer for making a fresh assessment;
[x=  41.27] [y= 126.20] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (c) in an appeal against the order of assessment for which the proceeding before the Settlement Commission abates under section 245HA of the Income-
[x=  55.20] [y= 135.75] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] tax Act, 1961 (43 of 1961), the Commissioner (Appeals) may, after taking into consideration all the material and other information produced by the
[x=  55.20] [y= 145.30] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] assessee before, or the results of the inquiry held or evidence recorded by, the Settlement Commission, in the course of the proceeding before it and
[x=  55.20] [y= 154.85] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] such other material as may be brought on his record, confirm, reduce, enhance or annul the assessment;
[x=  40.80] [y= 165.46] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (d) in an appeal against an order imposing a penalty, he may confirm or cancel such order or vary it so as either to enhance or to reduce the penalty;
[x=  41.27] [y= 176.06] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (e) in any other case, he may pass such orders in the appeal as he thinks fit.
[x=  27.62] [y= 187.73] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (2) The Joint Commissioner (Appeals) or the Commissioner (Appeals), shall not enhance an assessment or a penalty or reduce the amount of refund, unless
[x=  27.62] [y= 200.46] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] the appellant has had a reasonable opportunity of showing cause against such enhancement or reduction.
[x=  27.62] [y= 215.85] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (3) The Joint Commissioner (Appeals) or the Commissioner (Appeals), may consider and decide any matter arising out of the proceedings in which the
[x=  27.62] [y= 228.58] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] order appealed against was passed, irrespective of the fact that such matter was not raised before him by the appellant.
