[x=  27.62] [y=  65.20] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] Deduction of head office expenditure in case of non-residents.
[x=  27.62] [y=  80.59] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] 60. (1) Irrespective of anything to the contrary contained in sections 26 to 54, in the case of a non-resident assessee, deduction of head office expenditure
[x=  27.62] [y=  93.32] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] incurred by such assessee as is attributable to his business or profession in India, shall be allowed in computing the income chargeable under the head
[x=  27.62] [y= 106.05] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] "Profits and gains of business or profession" subject to provisions of sub-section (2).
[x=  27.62] [y= 121.96] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (2) The deduction allowable under sub-section (1) shall be restricted—
[x=  40.80] [y= 136.28] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (a) if the adjusted total income of the assessee is a loss, to an upper monetary limit of 5% of the average adjusted total income of the assessee; or
[x=  40.80] [y= 146.89] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (b) in any other case, to an upper monetary limit of 5% of the adjusted total income of the assessee.
[x=  27.62] [y= 158.56] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (3) For the purposes of this section,—
[x=  40.80] [y= 172.88] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (a) "adjusted total income" means the total income computed under this Act, without giving effect to the allowance referred to in this section or in
[x=  55.20] [y= 182.43] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] section 33(11) or the deduction referred to in section 32(i)(A) or any loss carried forward under section 111(1) or 112(1) or 113(2) or 115(2) or the
[x=  55.20] [y= 191.98] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] deductions under Chapter VIII;
[x=  40.80] [y= 202.59] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (b) "average adjusted total income" means,—
[x=  71.75] [y= 213.19] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (i) if the assessee is assessable for each of the three tax years immediately preceding the relevant tax year, the arithmetic mean of his adjusted
[x=  84.28] [y= 222.74] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] total income over those three tax years;
[x=  69.33] [y= 233.35] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (ii) if the assessee is assessable only for two of the said three tax years, the arithmetic mean of his adjusted total income over those two tax
[x=  84.28] [y= 242.90] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] years;
[x=  66.91] [y= 253.51] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (iii) if the assessee is assessable only for one of the said three tax years, his adjusted total income for that tax year;
[x=  41.27] [y= 264.11] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (c) "head office expenditure" means executive and general administration expenditure incurred by the assessee outside India, including expenditure
[x=  55.20] [y= 273.66] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] incurred in respect of—
[x=  71.75] [y= 284.27] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (i) rent, rates, taxes, repairs or insurance of any premises outside India used for the business or profession;
[x=  69.33] [y= 294.88] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (ii) salary, wages, annuity, pension, fees, bonus, commission, gratuity, perquisites or profits in lieu of, or in addition to, salary, whether paid or
[x=  84.28] [y= 304.43] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] allowed to any employee or other person employed in, or managing the affairs of, any office outside India;
[x=  66.91] [y= 315.04] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (iii) travelling by any employee or other person employed in, or managing the affairs of, any office outside India; and
[x=  67.93] [y= 325.64] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (iv) such other matters connected with executive and general administration, as may be prescribed.
