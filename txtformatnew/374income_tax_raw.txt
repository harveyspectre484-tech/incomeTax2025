[x=  27.62] [y=  65.20] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] Interpretation of "High Court".
[x=  27.62] [y=  80.59] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] 374. For the purposes of this Chapter, "High Court" means,—
[x=  42.68] [y=  95.44] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (i) for any State, the High Court for that State;
[x=  40.26] [y= 106.05] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (ii) for the Union territory of Jammu and Kashmir, the High Court of Jammu and Kashmir and Ladakh;
[x=  37.84] [y= 116.66] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (iii) for the Union territory of Ladakh, the High Court of Jammu and Kashmir and Ladakh;
[x=  38.85] [y= 127.26] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (iv) for the Union territory of the Andaman and Nicobar Islands, the High Court at Calcutta;
[x=  41.27] [y= 137.87] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (v) for the Union territory of Lakshadweep, the High Court of Kerala;
[x=  38.85] [y= 148.48] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (vi) for the Union territory of Chandigarh, the High Court of Punjab and Haryana;
[x=  36.43] [y= 159.09] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (vii) for the Union territories of Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu, the High Court at Bombay;
[x=  34.01] [y= 169.70] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (viii) for the Union territory of Puducherry, the High Court at Madras; and
[x=  38.85] [y= 180.31] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (ix) for the National Capital Territory of Delhi, the High Court of Delhi.
