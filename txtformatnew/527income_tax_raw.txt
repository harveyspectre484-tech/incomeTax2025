[x=  27.62] [y=  65.20] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] Power to make exemption, etc., in relation to participation in business of prospecting for, extraction, etc., of mineral oils.
[x=  27.62] [y=  80.59] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] 527. (1) If the Central Government is satisfied that it is necessary or expedient in the public interest, it may, by notification, make an exemption, reduction
[x=  27.62] [y=  93.32] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] in rate, or other modification of income-tax for any class of persons specified in sub-section (2) or in regard to the whole or any part of the income of such
[x=  27.62] [y= 106.05] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] class of persons or the status in which such class of persons or the members thereof are to be assessed on their income from the business referred to in sub-
[x=  27.62] [y= 118.78] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] section (2)(a), effective from tax year beginning on or after 1st April, 1992.
[x=  27.62] [y= 134.69] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (2) The persons referred to in sub-section (1) shall be the following:—
[x=  40.80] [y= 149.01] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (a) persons with whom the Central Government has entered into agreements for the association or participation of that Government, or any person
[x=  55.20] [y= 158.56] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] authorised by that Government in any business of prospecting for or extraction or production of mineral oils;
[x=  40.80] [y= 169.17] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (b) persons providing any services or facilities or supplying any ship, aircraft, machinery or plant (whether by sale or hire) for any business consisting
[x=  55.20] [y= 178.72] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] of the prospecting for or extraction or production of mineral oils carried on by that Government, or any person specified by that Government by
[x=  55.20] [y= 188.26] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] notification; and
[x=  41.27] [y= 198.87] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (c) employees of the persons referred to in clause (a) or (b).
[x=  27.62] [y= 210.54] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (3) Every notification issued under this section shall be laid before each House of Parliament.
[x=  27.62] [y= 225.92] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (4) For the purposes this section,—
[x=  40.80] [y= 240.78] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (a) "mineral oil" includes petroleum and natural gas;
[x=  40.80] [y= 251.38] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (b) "status" means the category of person as defined in section 2(77) under which the assessee is assessed.
