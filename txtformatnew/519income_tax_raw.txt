[x=  27.62] [y=  80.59] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] 519. (1) The Central Government may, if it is of the opinion that with a view to obtaining the evidence of any person appearing to have been directly or
[x=  27.62] [y=  93.32] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] indirectly concerned in or privy to the concealment of income or to the evasion of payment of tax on income it is necessary or expedient so to do, for
[x=  27.62] [y= 106.05] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] reasons to be recorded in writing, tender to such person,—
[x=  40.80] [y= 120.90] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (a) immunity from prosecution for any offence under this Act or under the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 (45 of 2023), or under any other Central Act
[x=  55.20] [y= 130.45] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] in force; and
[x=  40.80] [y= 141.06] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (b) from imposition of any penalty under this Act on condition of his making a full and true disclosure of the whole circumstances relating to the
[x=  55.20] [y= 150.60] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] concealment of income or evasion of payment of tax on income.
[x=  27.62] [y= 162.27] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (2) A tender of immunity made to, and accepted by, the person concerned, shall, to the extent to which the immunity extends, render him immune from
[x=  27.62] [y= 175.00] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] prosecution for any offence in respect of which the tender was made, or from the imposition of any penalty under this Act.
[x=  27.62] [y= 190.39] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (3) If it appears to the Central Government that any person to whom immunity has been tendered under this section––
[x=  40.80] [y= 204.71] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (a) has not complied with the conditions on which the tender was made; or
[x=  40.80] [y= 215.32] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (b) is wilfully concealing anything; or

[x=  27.62] [y= 237.59] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] the Central Government may record a finding to that effect, and thereupon the immunity shall be deemed to have been withdrawn.
[x=  27.62] [y= 253.51] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (4) The person whose immunity has been withdrawn under sub-section (3) may be tried for the offence in respect of which the tender of immunity was
