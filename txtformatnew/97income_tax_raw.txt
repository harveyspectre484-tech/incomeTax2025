[x=  27.62] [y=  65.20] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] Chargeability of income in transfer of assets.
[x=  27.62] [y=  80.59] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] 97. (1) All income arising to any person by virtue of a revocable transfer of assets shall be chargeable to income-tax as income of the transferor and shall be
[x=  27.62] [y=  93.32] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] included in his total income.
[x=  27.62] [y= 109.23] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (2) The provisions of sub-section (1) shall not apply,—
[x=  40.80] [y= 123.55] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (a) where a transfer is by way of trust which is not revocable during the lifetime of the beneficiary and in case of any other transfer, is not revocable
[x=  55.20] [y= 133.10] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] during the lifetime of the transferee; and
[x=  40.80] [y= 143.71] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (b) the transferor does not derive any direct or indirect benefit from such income in cases referred to in clause (a).
[x=  27.62] [y= 155.38] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (3) Irrespective of the provisions of sub-section (2), all income arising to any person by virtue of such transfer shall be chargeable to income-tax as income
[x=  27.62] [y= 168.11] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] of the transferor as and when the power to revoke such transfer arises, and shall then be included in his total income.
