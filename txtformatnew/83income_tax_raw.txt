[x=  27.62] [y=  65.20] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] Capital gains on transfer of land used for agricultural purposes not to be charged in certain cases.
[x=  27.62] [y=  80.59] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] 83. (1) Where an assessee, being an individual or a Hindu undivided family,—
[x=  40.80] [y=  95.44] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (a) has capital gains arising from the transfer of a capital asset, being land, which was used by the assessee or his parent, or the Hindu undivided family
[x=  55.20] [y= 104.99] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] for agricultural purposes (original asset), in two years immediately preceding the date of transfer; and
[x=  40.80] [y= 115.60] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (b) has, within two years after that date, purchased any other land for being used for agricultural purposes (new asset),
[x=  27.62] [y= 127.26] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] then, instead of the capital gains being charged to income-tax as income of the tax year in which the transfer took place, it shall be dealt with as follows:—
[x=  42.68] [y= 141.59] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (i) if the capital gains exceed the cost of the new asset, such excess shall be charged under section 67, and for computing any capital gains arising from
[x=  55.20] [y= 151.13] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] the transfer of the new asset within three years of its purchase, the cost shall be nil; or
[x=  40.26] [y= 161.74] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (ii) if the capital gains is equal to or less than the cost of the new asset, no capital gains shall be charged under section 67, and for computing any capital
[x=  55.20] [y= 171.29] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] gains arising from the transfer of the new asset within three years of its purchase, the cost shall be reduced by the amount of the capital gains.
[x=  27.62] [y= 182.96] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (2) If the capital gains referred to in sub-section (1) is not utilised by the assessee to purchase the new asset before filing the return of income under section
[x=  27.62] [y= 195.69] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] 263, then—
[x=  40.80] [y= 210.01] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (a) the unutilised amount shall be deposited in a specified bank or institution and utilised as per the scheme notified by the Central Government;
[x=  40.80] [y= 220.62] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (b) such deposit shall be made before the filing of the return and not later than the due date applicable in the case of the assessee for filing the return of

[x=  41.27] [y= 240.78] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (c) the proof of deposit shall be submitted along with such return.
[x=  27.62] [y= 252.45] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (3) For the purposes of sub-section (1), the amount already utilised for purchasing the new asset together with the deposited amount under sub-section (2),

[x=  27.62] [y= 281.09] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (4) If the amount deposited under sub-section (2) is not fully utilised for purchase of the new asset within the period specified in sub-section (1), then,—
