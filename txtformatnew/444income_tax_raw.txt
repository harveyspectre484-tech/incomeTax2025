[x=  27.62] [y=  65.20] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] Penalty for false entry, etc., in books of account.
[x=  27.62] [y=  80.59] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] 444. (1) The Assessing Officer or the Joint Commissioner (Appeals) or the Commissioner (Appeals), may impose a penalty equal to the aggregate amount
[x=  27.62] [y=  93.32] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] of false or omitted entry, where during any proceeding under this Act, it is found that in the books of account maintained by any person there is—
[x=  40.80] [y= 108.17] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (a) a false entry; or
[x=  40.80] [y= 118.78] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (b) an omission of any entry which is relevant for computation of total income of such person, to evade tax liability.
[x=  27.62] [y= 130.45] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (2) Without prejudice to sub-section (1), the Assessing Officer or the Joint Commissioner (Appeals) or the Commissioner (Appeals) may impose a penalty
[x=  27.62] [y= 143.18] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] equal to the aggregated amount of false or omitted entry, on any other person, who causes the person referred to in the said sub-section in any manner to
[x=  27.62] [y= 155.91] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] make a false entry or omits or causes to omit any entry referred to in that sub-section.
[x=  27.62] [y= 171.29] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (3) For the purposes of this section, the expression "false entry" includes use or intention to use—
[x=  40.80] [y= 185.61] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (a) forged or falsified documents such as a false invoice or, in general, a false piece of documentary evidence; or
[x=  40.80] [y= 196.22] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (b) invoice in respect of supply or receipt of goods or services or both issued by the person or any other person without actual supply or receipt of such
[x=  55.20] [y= 205.77] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] goods or services, or both; or
[x=  41.27] [y= 216.38] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (c) invoice in respect of supply or receipt of goods or services or both to or from a person who does not exist.
