[x=  27.62] [y=  96.50] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] 331. Irrespective of anything contained in the Limited Liability Partnership Act, 2008 (6 of 2009), where any tax including penalty, interest, fee or any
[x=  27.62] [y= 109.23] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] other sum payable under the Act is due and cannot be recovered from—
[x=  40.80] [y= 123.55] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (a) the limited liability partnership in respect of any income of any tax year; or
[x=  40.80] [y= 134.16] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (b) any other person in respect of any income of any tax year during which such other person was a limited liability partnership,
[x=  27.62] [y= 145.83] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] then, in such case, every such person who was a partner of such limited liability partnership at any time during the relevant tax year, shall be jointly and
[x=  27.62] [y= 158.56] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] severally liable for the payment of such tax due unless he proves that the non-recovery cannot be attributed to any gross neglect, misfeasance or breach of
[x=  27.62] [y= 171.29] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] duty on his part in relation to the affairs of the limited liability partnership.
