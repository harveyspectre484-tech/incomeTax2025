[x=  27.62] [y=  65.20] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] Presumption as to culpable mental state.
[x=  27.62] [y=  80.59] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] 490. (1) In any prosecution for any offence under this Act, which requires a culpable mental state on the part of the accused, the court shall presume the
[x=  27.62] [y=  93.32] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] existence of such mental state but it shall be a defence for the accused to prove the fact that he had no such mental state with respect to the Act charged as
[x=  27.62] [y= 106.05] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] an offence in that prosecution.
[x=  27.62] [y= 121.96] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (2) For the purposes of this section, the expression "culpable mental state" includes intention, motive or knowledge of a fact or belief in, or reason to
[x=  27.62] [y= 134.69] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] believe, a fact.
[x=  27.62] [y= 150.07] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (3) For the purposes of this section, a fact is said to be proved only when the court believes it to exist beyond reasonable doubt and not merely when its
[x=  27.62] [y= 162.80] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] existence is established by a preponderance of probability.
