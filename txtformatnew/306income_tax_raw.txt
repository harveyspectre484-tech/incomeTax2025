[x=  27.62] [y=  80.59] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] Who may be regarded as agent.
[x=  27.62] [y=  96.50] [font= 8.5] [bold=True] 306. (1) For the purposes of this Act, "agent", in relation to a non-resident, includes—
[x=  40.80] [y= 110.82] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (a) any person in India—
[x=  71.75] [y= 121.43] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (i) who is employed by or on behalf of the non-resident; or
[x=  69.33] [y= 132.04] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (ii) who has any business connection with the non-resident; or
[x=  66.91] [y= 142.65] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (iii) from or through whom the non-resident is in receipt of any income, whether directly or indirectly; or
[x=  67.93] [y= 153.26] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (iv) who is the trustee of the non-resident;
[x=  40.80] [y= 163.86] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (b) any other person who, whether a resident or non-resident, has acquired by means of a transfer, a capital asset in India.
[x=  27.62] [y= 175.53] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (2) A broker in India who, in respect of any transactions, does not deal directly with or on behalf of a non-resident principal but deals with or through a non-
[x=  27.62] [y= 188.26] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] resident broker shall not be deemed to be an agent under this section in respect of such transactions, if the following conditions are fulfilled:—
[x=  40.80] [y= 202.59] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (a) the transactions are carried on in the ordinary course of business through the first-mentioned broker; and
[x=  40.80] [y= 213.19] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (b) the non-resident broker is carrying on such transactions in the ordinary course of his business and not as a principal.
[x=  27.62] [y= 224.86] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (3) A person shall not be treated as the agent of a non-resident unless he has had an opportunity of being heard by the Assessing Officer as to his liability to
[x=  27.62] [y= 237.59] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] be treated as such.
[x=  27.62] [y= 253.51] [font= 8.5] [bold=False] (4) For the purposes of this section, "business connection" shall have the meaning assigned to it in section 9(9)(a).
