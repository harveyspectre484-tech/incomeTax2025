**Definition of "tax year".**

- **3.** (1) For the purposes of this Act, "tax year" means the twelve months period of the financial year commencing on the 1st April.

- (2) In the case of a business or profession newly set up, or a source of income newly coming into existence in any financial year, the tax year shall be the

period beginning with—

   - (_a_) the date of setting up of such business or profession; or

   - (_b_) the date on which such source of income newly comes into existence,

and ending with the said financial year.
